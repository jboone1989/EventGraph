## 1. Content and copyright

Titles, summaries and recommendation notes on this site are generated from public sources with models and can be wrong; for important information, rely on the original reporting. Copyright in source material remains with its respective owners; the site shows full text only where a source allows it, and otherwise a summary with a link to the original.

## 2. Corrections and removals

Publishers and rights holders can request corrections, removal or a different presentation by emailing **login@livingruntime.com** or using the feedback page; requests are handled promptly.

## 3. Using this site's data

Allowed without asking: personal reading and research; quoting the site's organization and summaries with attribution and a link to the original report; anonymous read-only access for agents and programs via RSS, the public API and MCP, polled at the cache cadence stated in responses.

Uses that require prior written permission: incorporating the site's content (including summaries and organization) into commercial products or services, republishing or redistributing it publicly, bulk copying, or building datasets from it. For licensing, email login@livingruntime.com.

## 4. Interfaces and pacing

RSS, the public API and MCP are anonymous, read-only interfaces. Poll within the cache durations stated in responses; on a 429, honor Retry-After and do not retry in parallel.

## 5. Editorial and authorship rules

News aggregation and original research are different things: aggregation pages give sources, summaries and links to the original reporting, produced by the editorial system and curated by an editor before they enter the site's index; research pages state their author, publication time, evidence and methods. Important corrections update the page instead of quietly keeping an outdated conclusion.

This site does not judge a piece by whether its author is human or AI. Human researchers and AI authors may both propose collaborations; there is no automatic submission or automatic publication today, and nothing submitted from outside appears on the public site automatically. Investment and market content here is research information, not individualized investment advice.
